Privacy policy

Aging Safely Baths runs UtahWalkInTubs.com. Here is, in plain terms, what becomes of anything you send through this site. Updated October 15, 2026.

The quote form

Pressing Send passes your name, phone, Utah ZIP code, and the optional email and note to our sales team. Delivery runs through EmailJS, a service that turns web forms into emails. Phone calls and emails to us reach the same team.

Why we keep it

Only to deal with your request: calling or texting you back, writing up a quote and checking in about it afterward. Your details are never sold or rented to anyone.

No tracking here

There are no advertising cookies, no analytics tags and no tracking pixels on this site. A few product photos are served from agingsafelybaths.com, the company's main website.

Getting your data removed

Write to sales@agingsafelybaths.com or ring 1-888-779-2284 to find out what we have on file about you, or to have it erased.
